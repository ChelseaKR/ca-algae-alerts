## Description

> Toxic algae (harmful algal blooms) can make people sick and can kill dogs.
> California's Water Boards and Missouri's Department of Natural Resources
> record advisories for lakes, rivers and shorelines. This app puts those
> records on your iPhone, one lake at a time, with every advisory on file.
>
> FOR EVERYONE AT THE WATER
> Swimmers, families, paddlers, anglers and dog owners: see which waters
> have an advisory on file, when it was posted, and what its level asks of
> people, children and pets. Each lake has a section on dogs, which are more
> exposed than people because they swim through scum and drink the water.
>
> HONEST ABOUT WHAT THE RECORDS SAY
> • An advisory stays listed, with its date, until the state records an
>   end. Old advisories say "no all-clear recorded".
> • When the state's latest record recommends no advisory, the app says so
>   with its date, and that it is not a finding that the water is safe now.
> • No advisory does not mean no algae: most waters are never tested.
>
> FREE
> • Browse and search every lake and river in the state's records
> • Current advisories first, each with its posted date
> • Every lake's history, spot by spot (California since 2016, Missouri
>   since 2017)
> • For large lakes, EPA's weekly satellite estimate, clearly labeled: it
>   is not an advisory and does not measure toxins
> • Favorites
>
> FULL ACCESS, ONE PURCHASE
> • Alerts when a favorite lake gets a new advisory or its level changes,
>   usually within about a day of the state publishing it
> • A Home Screen and Lock Screen widget for your favorite lakes
>
> PRIVATE BY DESIGN
> No account, no analytics, no tracking. Your favorites stay on your iPhone.
>
> Data: California State Water Resources Control Board, Freshwater Harmful
> Algal Bloom Program, via data.ca.gov (public domain); Missouri Department
> of Natural Resources (used under the State of Missouri Data Policy, with
> its required disclaimer in the app); U.S. EPA Cyanobacteria Assessment
> Network satellite estimates. This app is independent and is not
> affiliated with or endorsed by any state or federal agency. Advisories are
> recommendations, and reporting is voluntary. Always check signs posted at
> the water.
